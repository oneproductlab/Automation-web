## "Foundation" is the word that matters

The client called it a foundation, and that is the distinction worth drawing
out. A brochure site is finished the day it ships. A foundation is the thing
you keep building on — which means the decisions that matter are the ones about
what comes next, not what launches.

Built the second way, the agency can add functionality as it grows. Built the
first way, the first real requirement means starting again.
